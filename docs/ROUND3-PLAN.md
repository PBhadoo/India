# Round 3 plan: INC-led vs BJP-led head-to-head (agreed)

Decisions: other Centre parties are collapsed, not deleted; the site presents evidence and arguments and never declares a winner; this runs after the SIR/institutions/business/CJP round has merged.

1. Centre headline shows two blocs, INC-led and BJP-led, with coalition partners rolled into the lead bloc (UPA, NDA). Governments led by other parties (Janata, Janata Dal, National Front, United Front, Samajwadi Janata, caretakers) sit in a collapsed "Other governments" group. The partner-by-partner split stays available as a toggle. Tenure totals keep all years.
2. Head-to-head view by category and period: what each bloc did, measured outcomes, same template for both.
3. `reform` block on every action: attributed points under keep / change / repeal (court, CAG, RBI, committee, economists, either party's own members). Equal quotas for old and current government policies.
4. Indicator comparison by period (growth, inflation, jobs, deficits, poverty, health, schooling, infrastructure) from official data, with global context and attribution caveats.
5. Manifesto promise tracker: INC and BJP promises per election, status (delivered / partly / not), sources.
6. Open questions page: live reform debates with the strongest case for each option and who holds it; no verdict.
7. Neutrality review with equal point counts per side per topic, then blind re-scoring where entries change.
