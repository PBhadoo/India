# Source quality: where to verify first

Derived from each entry's `scoring.confidence`, its source types and notes in its body. "Primary" counts sources typed primary, official-data or court. Sources whose publisher is Wikipedia, a think-tank (including PRS bill tracks and briefs), a party site, an exam-prep site, a law-firm or legal-news summary are typed `reputable-media`, not primary. Mirrors that host the actual text (India Code, GlobalSecurity copies of PIB releases, ADB treaty copy, constitution.org) keep their type.

Entries with no primary source were set to confidence low and carry a "Source note" in the body. Entries with a body note about unopened or unverified documents rely partly on search-result summaries.
