## In depth

Delhi hosted the Ninth Asian Games in November and December 1982. Preparation began in earnest after 1979, leaving a short construction period. The approved expenditure was reported to Parliament at Rs 54.83 crore, with additional sums from other agencies and a grant from Kuwait for an indoor stadium.

The Games are also noted for the introduction of colour television. The Cabinet decided to broadcast in colour, Doordarshan bought outside broadcasting vans, and colour sets were imported. Sources differ on the number of sets imported (50,000 permitted as of November 1982 against a later reference to one lakh).

The Supreme Court decision of September 1982 arose from a petition by a civil liberties group about conditions of workers on Games sites. The court held that payment below the minimum wage amounted to forced labour and directed authorities to ensure minimum wages. It is also cited in the development of public interest litigation.

Official total cost figures and venue utilisation data were not retrieved; the 1980 and 1982 figures in Parliament differ, so confidence is low. Medal counts are from secondary sources.
