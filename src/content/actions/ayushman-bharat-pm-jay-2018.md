## In depth

PM-JAY provides publicly funded cover for hospital treatment to eligible families and is administered by the National Health Authority. In October 2024 it was extended to all citizens aged 70 and above, with cover of Rs 5 lakh per year.

Administrative data show a large and growing number of authorised admissions. These data measure use, not health outcomes or reductions in household spending, and the sources reviewed here do not contain a national impact evaluation. The CAG's 2023 audit examined the first period of the scheme and raised issues about the beneficiary database and claims controls; the Ministry said some of the media characterisations were misleading and responded to the audit recommendations.

NFHS-5 shows that about two in five households had some member covered by a health insurance or financing scheme in 2019-21. This includes state and employer schemes, not only PM-JAY, and the survey was conducted before the 2024 expansion, so it should not be read as a measure of PM-JAY alone.

Assessments of the scheme therefore rest on three kinds of evidence: scale of use, audit findings on controls, and coverage gaps. Longer-run evidence on financial protection and health outcomes is limited in the sources reviewed.
