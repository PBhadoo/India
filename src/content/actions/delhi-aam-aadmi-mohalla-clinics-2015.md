## In depth

The Mohalla Clinic model placed small, free primary-care clinics in neighbourhoods, with a doctor, medicines and basic tests. The first opened in July 2015, and the government set a target of 1,000.

The best-documented independent assessment is the CAG performance audit tabled in February 2025, which examined the years to March 2023. It reported 523 clinics against the target, gaps in staffing, facilities and inspections, and short consultation times. AAP disputed parts of it, including the clinic count. The audit was tabled under a different government than the one that ran the scheme, a context readers may weigh.

The CAG audit covered inputs and processes; the sources opened do not report patient health outcomes or the effect on hospital crowding, so those effects are not assessed. The full CAG report PDF was not opened; its listing page and press accounts were used.
