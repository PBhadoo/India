## In depth

Delhi was among the first states to hand electricity distribution to private operators. The Delhi Vidyut Board was unbundled in 2002, and three distribution companies were formed, with private operators holding 51% and the Delhi government 49%.

Two documents opened here take different positions. A former BSES chairman, who worked on the reforms, credits the change with cutting losses from over 55% to about 7% and supporting growth in demand. A CSEP policy blog, written in 2025, accepts the loss reduction but argues that disputes over tariffs and deferred costs leave consumers and the regulator in a long-running stalemate.

No government order, DERC order or CAG report was opened. The Delhi power department's own page appeared only in search results, and loss figures differ by source and year. The confidence rating is therefore low, and the entry does not estimate the effect on consumer tariffs over time.
