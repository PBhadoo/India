## In depth

The measure withdrew the two highest-value notes with a few hours' notice and replaced them over several months with new Rs 500 and Rs 2,000 notes. The government stated that the aim was to curb black money, counterfeit currency and terror financing. The RBI later reported that almost all withdrawn notes were deposited, which different commentators read in different ways: as evidence that unaccounted cash largely entered the banking system, or as showing that the scheme extinguished little cash.

Economic effects are measured differently. Headline GDP data showed a small dip, while a district-level study found short-term contractions in activity and credit that headline series may not fully capture. Digital payments grew quickly afterwards, though other policies and private platforms also contributed. On the legal question, the Supreme Court majority upheld the scheme on proportionality and consultation grounds, and the dissent held that the statutory route was not followed.
