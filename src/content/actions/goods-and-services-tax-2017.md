## In depth

GST involved three governments across about 17 years. A design committee began work in 2000, the idea was announced in the 2006 Budget, a 2011 Bill lapsed, and the 2016 amendment passed. The Council structure gives States two-thirds of the votes in a body chaired by the Union Finance Minister, with decisions needing a three-fourths majority; opposition-ruled States have sought revenue protection during rate changes.

Collections and registrations have grown, and the government credits the tax with a unified market. Critics point to multiple rates, compliance load for small firms, early technical issues documented by the CAG, and revenue risk to States. The September 2025 restructuring simplified rates but carries an estimated revenue cost.

The origin credit (originGovernmentId) is set to the Vajpayee government based on the 2000 committee; the 2006 announcement and 2011 Bill were under the Manmohan Singh governments, and sources disagree on some dates for the Empowered Committee. Statistics on collections come from press reports of government releases; the PIB text itself was not opened.
