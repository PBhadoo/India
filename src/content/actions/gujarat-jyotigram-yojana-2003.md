## In depth

Jyotigram Yojana changed how rural electricity was wired. Farm pumps and village households had shared a single feeder, so farm supply was rationed to limit use across the whole feeder. The scheme laid separate lines so that villages could be given a continuous supply while farms received a fixed daily schedule.

Evidence points in different directions. A health-facility study finds better electricity availability. A satellite-luminosity study finds no overall consumption increase. Reported loss reductions from a bank report cannot be separated from other power-sector changes of the period. No primary Gujarat government record was opened for this entry; the programme description comes from a secondary summary.

The scheme was later promoted nationally. Studies that compare farm households or groundwater use, mentioned in search results, were not opened.
