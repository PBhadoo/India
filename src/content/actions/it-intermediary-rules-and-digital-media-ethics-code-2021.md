## In depth

The 2021 Rules link a platform's legal immunity for user content to compliance with due-diligence duties, add obligations for large platforms, and bring online news publishers and streaming services under a code of ethics. The Union government presented the Rules as an accountability framework; critics, including PRS and several petitioners, questioned whether delegated legislation could go this far and whether Ministry-level oversight of digital news is compatible with press independence.

Parts of the Rules have been tested in court. The 2023 fact-check amendment was struck down by the Bombay High Court after a split verdict, with the judges reading Article 19(1)(a) and the proportionality test differently; the Supreme Court appeal is reported pending and its current status was not verified. Other challenges to the 2021 Rules, including in the Delhi High Court, were also reported but not reviewed in detail.

No official compliance or enforcement statistics were located, so the benefits above are described by design rather than measured outcome.
