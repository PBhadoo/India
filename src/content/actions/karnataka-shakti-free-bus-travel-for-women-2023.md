## In depth

Shakti was the first of five guarantees promised by the Congress in the 2023 Karnataka election campaign, which the party's government implemented after taking office. Women can ride state-run non-AC buses within Karnataka at no charge on showing an ID.

The government presents the scheme as supporting women's rights and the guarantees as investments. Budget and audit documents, as summarised by PRS, show the five guarantee schemes together at about Rs 51,000 crore a year and report an audit finding that they raised the revenue deficit and reduced infrastructure spending in 2023-24.

This entry does not weigh the guarantees' separate effects on women's mobility or employment, because the sources opened do not report such outcomes. The cost figures for Shakti alone come from press reports of official data rather than from budget documents opened directly.
