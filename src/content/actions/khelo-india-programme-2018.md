## In depth

The Khelo India scheme, revamped from 2017-18, supports competitions at school and university level, sports infrastructure and a programme of support for selected athletes. Funding has risen over successive approvals: Rs 1,756 crore for 2017-20, Rs 3,790.50 crore approved in 2021, and Rs 29,054 crore approved for 2026-31 within a larger outlay.

The government reports that a significant share of recent national contingents consists of athletes identified through the scheme, such as 28 of 117 at Paris 2024. Whether this indicates the scheme caused those athletes' progress or reflects their selection is not established by the sources reviewed. India's medal ranking at Paris 2024 was lower than at Tokyo 2020.

The exact start date is described differently across sources, so the entry uses 2018 as the year of the first Khelo India Games. Several details, such as athlete numbers and the scholarship amount, could not be verified and are omitted. The independent evaluation of outcomes is a key gap, so confidence is low.
