## In depth

The four Codes were passed in 2019-2020 under the second Modi government and brought into force in November 2025. They merge 29 central laws into codes on wages, industrial relations, social security and workplace safety. Labour is on the Concurrent List, so State rules are required for full operation, and by early 2026 these were incomplete in several States.

The government states that the Codes simplify compliance and extend coverage. Employer groups and advisers generally welcome consolidation, while the central trade unions object to provisions on retrenchment thresholds, strike notice and union registration. Because the Codes are recent, outcome data on employment, wages and coverage are not yet available, and the confidence rating is low.

The PLFS figures cited describe the base the Codes apply to. Several source details here come from law-firm and explainer pages; the Gazette notifications and the PIB release were not opened directly.
