## In depth

Maharashtra's scheme is often discussed as a forerunner of later national rural employment programmes. It began in 1972 during a drought, using public works for unskilled rural labour, and was tied to a state-level tax on professions and employment from 1975. A 1977 Act gave it statutory form and a dedicated fund.

This entry rests mainly on catalogue records of the Planning Commission's joint evaluation and on search-result summaries of statutes and audits. The evaluation's findings, the statute texts and the audit chapter were not opened, so the facts about financing and the audit findings are provisional and the confidence is low.

A later study, summarised in search results only, reports that participation fell over 1980-97 as irrigation expanded farm work. A reader wanting to assess outcomes should consult the 1977 PEO report and the later academic reviews directly.
