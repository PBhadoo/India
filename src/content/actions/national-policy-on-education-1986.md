## In depth

The policy's stated focus was removing disparities in educational opportunity, particularly for women and for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. It also set up programmes meant to put physical resources into primary schools (Operation Blackboard), establish residential schools for talented rural children (Navodaya Vidyalayas) and widen access through open and distance learning (IGNOU).

In 1992, under a different government, the policy was modified on the recommendation of a CABE committee, and a revised Programme of Action was issued. The 1986 policy remained the reference document until the National Education Policy 2020 replaced it.
