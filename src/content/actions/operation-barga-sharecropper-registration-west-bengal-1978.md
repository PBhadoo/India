- The Left Front government led by Jyoti Basu (CPI(M)) took office on 21 June 1977. Operation Barga began in 1978; sources give different start months (June 1978 in the Wikipedia summary [3], September 1978 in a table in [1], October 1978 in [2]), so the date field uses 1 June 1978 as approximate.

## In depth

Operation Barga was an administrative drive to register sharecroppers under existing tenancy law. Registration gave a recorded sharecropper protection from eviction and a legal share of the crop. By the state's own data to 2003, about 1.16 million sharecroppers were recorded after September 1978.

Evaluations ask different questions. Survey evidence in [1] suggests most registered bargadars kept their land, and the share of contracts at the legal 75% rose. The same data show landlord input-sharing fell sharply, which a critic had predicted. Estimates of the programme's effect on output range from about 5% to about a quarter of post-1979 growth, depending on the study.

No primary government document was opened for this entry; the official figures are quoted through a published academic paper that cites them. Present-day debate concerns whether registrations should lapse when a cultivator stops farming, which proponents of change and defenders of sharecroppers' rights weigh differently [2].
