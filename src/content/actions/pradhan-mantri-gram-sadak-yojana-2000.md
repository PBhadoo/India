## In depth

PMGSY was started on 25 December 2000 to build all-weather roads to rural habitations not yet connected. The first phase set population thresholds, with 1,000 people by 2003 and 500 by 2007 for the plains and lower thresholds for hill, tribal and desert areas. Later phases and a road connectivity project for areas affected by left-wing extremism followed.

Government reporting shows near-complete construction for phase I habitations as of July 2025 and about 95% physical progress on sanctioned road length by December 2025. These figures are from the programme's own reporting. A 2017 parliamentary committee figure of 65% is lower because it uses a different count of eligible habitations, so the two should not be compared directly.

An academic study cited on Wikipedia found that new roads helped workers leave agriculture but did not change farm incomes or assets in a major way. The extent of road quality problems was not assessed because the audit summary found in search was not opened. Readers should regard the coverage figures as official claims.
