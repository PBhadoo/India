## In depth

Proposals to give public broadcasting autonomy date from the Verghese Committee of 1977. Parliament passed the Prasar Bharati Bill in 1990 and the President assented on 12 September 1990, but it did not come into force until the Gujral government notified it in 1997.

Supporters see the Act as the statutory basis for editorial independence of public broadcasters. Critics note that the Centre retains powers of direction, that board appointments involve the government, and that funding disputes have recurred. The sources reviewed disagree on some dates (notification versus formation) and none were official Gazette copies, so confidence is low.

The Cable Television Networks (Regulation) Act, 1995 and the Supreme Court's 1995 ruling on airwaves are related developments not covered in this entry.
