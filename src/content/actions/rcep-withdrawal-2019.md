## In depth

RCEP negotiations began in 2012 among the ten ASEAN members and six partners including India. India took part until November 2019, when the Prime Minister announced it would not join. The stated reasons were that the text did not address core concerns, with reporting highlighting import surges, rules of origin and market access, especially with respect to China.

The remaining 15 members signed the agreement on 15 November 2020 and it took effect on 1 January 2022. The group stated that India could join later.

Supporters of the decision argue it protected sectors such as dairy, agriculture and small manufacturing from import competition, and preserved bargaining room. Critics argue India missed access to a large trading area and value chains; the Petri and Plummer analysis projected losses for India. Evidence on the counterfactual is modelled rather than observed, so scoring should treat outcomes as uncertain. One figure sometimes cited for the share of India's deficit accounted for by RCEP members was not verified here.
