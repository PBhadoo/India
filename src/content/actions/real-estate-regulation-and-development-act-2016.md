## In depth

RERA moved homebuyer protection from consumer courts alone to a registered-project model with State regulators. The 2013 Bill was reworked after a Standing Committee review and passed in March 2016. The Act covers registration, disclosure, carpet-area pricing, deposit of buyers' funds in a separate account and time-bound complaints.

Supporters point to the number of registrations and complaints disposed and to the Supreme Court's 2021 judgment. Concerns raised include different pace of implementation across States, exemptions for smaller or ongoing projects, and limited public data on pending cases. Delivery of possession and price outcomes are not measured in the sources used here, so conclusions on whether delays fell are not drawn.

The exact deposit percentage in the final Act and the project-size exemption thresholds were not verified from the sources opened; the 70% figure above is from PRS's description of the Bill.
