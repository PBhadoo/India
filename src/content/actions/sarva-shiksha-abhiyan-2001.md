## In depth

Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan was launched at the start of 2001 as a Centrally Sponsored Scheme to bring all children aged 6 to 14 into elementary education, building on the District Primary Education Programme begun in 1993-94. After the 86th Amendment made elementary education a Fundamental Right, and the RTE Act took effect in April 2010, SSA became the main programmatic vehicle for implementing the Act. Its framework was revised in March 2011.

The sources opened for this entry describe enrolment and dropout trends and some state audit findings. A 2016 parliamentary committee report concluded that enrolment was near universal but that learning outcomes were unsatisfactory. State audits from 2005 recorded children still out of school in West Bengal and Maharashtra.

Evidence on funding totals, learning achievement over the whole period and the exact launch date was not collected, so the date field is a placeholder for January 2001 and confidence is low. SSA was later merged into the Samagra Shiksha scheme, which this entry does not assess.
