## In depth

After independence, states were organised as Parts A, B, C and D, based on provinces and princely states. A 1948 commission rejected language as a criterion, but agitation, notably for a Telugu-speaking state, led to the creation of Andhra State in 1953. The government then appointed the States Reorganisation Commission in December 1953.

The Commission reported on 30 September 1955. According to an explainer cited on Wikipedia, it proposed 16 states and three centrally administered areas, while the government adopted 14 states and 6 union territories. Parliament passed the Act on 31 August 1956 and it took effect on 1 November 1956, with the Seventh Amendment replacing the old classification.
