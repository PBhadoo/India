## In depth

Tamil Nadu had run school meals in various forms for decades before 1982, including a Kamaraj-era extension in the 1960s. The 1982 decision by the AIADMK government under M. G. Ramachandran made a cooked noon meal available to children in government primary classes across the state, starting in rural areas on 1 July and in urban areas in September.

The official aims listed are nutrition, literacy, enrolment and fewer dropouts. Research summarised in a working paper reports a positive enrolment effect from noon meal schemes, though it also notes that most evaluations rely on primary surveys. This entry could not retrieve Tamil Nadu-specific outcome figures from a primary evaluation, so the confidence rating is low.

The scheme's later history includes repeated changes in the department running it and the extension of cooked meals nationwide after the Supreme Court's 2001 direction. This entry does not assess the 2022 Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme.
