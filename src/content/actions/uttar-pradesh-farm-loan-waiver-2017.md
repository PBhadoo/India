## In depth

The waiver was among the first major decisions of the government that took office in Uttar Pradesh in March 2017. It cancelled crop loans up to Rs 1 lakh per farmer for small and marginal farmers, with the cost to be met through state bonds.

Reports disagree on how many farmers benefit and on the split of the Rs 36,359 crore total. The RBI Governor spoke against loan waivers in general two days later, without naming the state. A commentator at ORF argued for the waiver on humanitarian grounds but also said waivers bring only temporary relief and that irrigation, credit access and non-farm jobs are the structural issues.

This entry relies on press and commentary only. No government order, budget document, CAG report or RBI data on repayment outcomes was opened, so outcomes such as later farm credit behaviour are not assessed and confidence is low.
