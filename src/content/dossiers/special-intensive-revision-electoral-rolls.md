## In depth

On 24 June 2025 the Election Commission of India (ECI) ordered a Special Intensive Revision of the Bihar electoral roll, about four months before the state's Assembly election, in the term of the NDA government (centre-2024-modi-3). The ECI is an independent constitutional body; its power over rolls derives from Article 324 and, as the Supreme Court later held, section 21(3) of the Representation of the People Act, 1950. This entry could not confirm from an opened source whether the order also cites rule 25 of the Registration of Electors Rules, 1960.

The SIR differs from the usual annual summary revision. Electors received pre-printed enumeration forms from Booth Level Officers (BLOs), and the roll was rebuilt from those forms. Electors traceable to the previous intensive roll (2003 in Bihar) did not need to submit documents; others were asked for one of eleven listed documents, with a different set of requirements depending on date of birth. The Supreme Court directed on 8 September 2025 that Aadhaar be accepted as proof of identity but not of citizenship.

Figures at each stage appear in the tables above. In Bihar the roll fell from 7.89 crore to about 7.42 crore; Phase 2 and Phase 3 draft and final rolls show larger percentage reductions in several States, mainly attributed by the ECI to absent, shifted, dead and duplicate entries.

Petitioners and opposition parties allege exclusion of eligible voters, short timelines and BLO overwork. The ECI says the exercise protects the purity of the roll and that claims and appeals remain open. On 27 May 2026 the Supreme Court upheld the SIR with limits on the ECI's citizenship inquiry. Proceedings on West Bengal appeals continue, with a hearing listed on 16 October 2026.

Status by phase on 12 October 2026: Bihar complete (final roll 30 September 2025); Phase 2 final rolls published in the listed States and UTs, with West Bengal appeals still before tribunals; Phase 3 under way, with the Karnataka and Maharashtra final rolls reported as scheduled for 27 October and 4 November 2026 (unverified against ECI releases).
